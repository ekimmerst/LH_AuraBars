<h1 style="color: #d9a94b;">LH AuraBars</h1>

<p>Track player and target buffs, debuffs, and spell cooldowns in compact, movable bars that fit the default WoW UI.</p>

<p>A tidy place for the buffs, debuffs and spell cooldowns you want to keep an eye on. LH AuraBars uses compact bars styled to sit naturally beside the rest of the LH UI, instead of covering your screen with another large panel.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">What it does</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Shows relevant player and target auras in separate groups, with support for classes, races and factions.</li>
  <li>Shows player buffs while a target exists, including when you target yourself; hides them without a target.</li>
  <li>Shows timed target buffs regardless of how long they have left; target debuffs keep the three-minute display limit.</li>
  <li>Tracks spell cooldowns alongside buffs and debuffs in the same compact style.</li>
  <li>Lets you move and resize the player and target groups separately, or keep their sizes synchronized.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Make it yours</h2>

<p>Open <strong>Options → AddOns → LH Tweaks → LH AuraBars</strong>. Unlock the bars to see previews, drag each group into place and use the small handle to resize it. Lock them when you're happy. The settings page also has a layout reset button.</p>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Settings profiles</h2>

<p>Choose a character under <strong>Use settings from</strong> to share both bar positions, sizes, synchronization and lock state. Configure that character and log out first. LH Tweaks can apply its selected source to all LH addons at once. Learned aura and cooldown duration data stays with each character. Choose <strong>This character</strong> to restore your own layout.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">More LH addons</h2>

<p>Choose only the pieces you want. Most companion addons require LH Tweaks; LH NameplateQuestInfo also works without it.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>LH CooldownPulse:</strong> Flashes an ability icon when its cooldown finishes.</li>
  <li><strong>LH ExpBar:</strong> A minimal experience bar with rested XP.</li>
  <li><strong>LH HideMe:</strong> Hides selected parts of the default UI, individually or all at once.</li>
  <li><strong>LH JobsDone:</strong> Plays the Warcraft peasant voice when a quest is ready to turn in.</li>
  <li><strong>LH MailBags:</strong> Opens your bags at the mailbox, with optional closing.</li>
  <li><strong>LH MP5:</strong> Shows an estimated five-second timer on the player mana bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH NameplateQuestInfo:</strong> Adds quest markers and objective progress to nameplates.</li>
  <li><strong>LH NameplateThreatColors:</strong> Colors Blizzard nameplates by threat and role.</li>
  <li><strong>LH PetStats:</strong> Shows hunter pet loyalty beside the pet portrait.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Range:</strong> Shows melee range, configurable spell-range zones for every class, and a live non-Mana resource bar in melee range.</li>
  <li><strong>LH BuffReminders:</strong> Reminds you when selected class buffs are missing.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Skills:</strong> Shows profession ranks in a movable compact bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Stats:</strong> Shows money, free bag slots and remaining ammo below the minimap.</li>
  <li><strong>LH SwingTimer:</strong> Shows main-hand and ranged swing timers below the player cast bar.</li>
  <li><strong>LH TinyDPS:</strong> Shows built-in damage meter data in a compact separate window.</li>
  <li><strong>LH Tweaks:</strong> Provides the shared settings hub plus chat, positioning, merchant and fishing tweaks.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #d9a94b;">Installation and feedback</h2>

<p><strong>LH Tweaks is required</strong> and must be installed alongside LH AuraBars. I originally made this for my own UI, but I'm glad if it fits yours too. If you find an aura that is missing or behaves oddly, or have an improvement in mind, please tell me in the CurseForge comments.</p>
